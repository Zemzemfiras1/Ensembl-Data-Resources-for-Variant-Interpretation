# Ensembl Data Resources for Variant Interpretation

## Workshop

This repository contains the materials for the **Ensembl Data Resources for Variant Interpretation** workshop as a part of the Clinical Bioinformatics for Variant Interpretation in Medical Genomics course | Day 1 | 06 October 2026

> The training is supported by the International Centre for Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (ICGEB),  organised by the Department of Genetics, Farhat Hached University Hospital & the Vice Deanery of Research at the Faculty of Medicine of Sousse 

## Contents

* `00-Ensembl-ICGEB-slide.pdf` - Workshop presentation slides.
* `01-Ensembl-ICGEB-instructions.pdf` - Workshop instructions and practical exercises.
* `02-Answer-Ensembl-ICGEB-instructions.pdf` - Answers and solutions to the workshop exercises.

## How to Use

1. Read the presentation slides to review the workshop topics.
2. Follow the instructions in the practical exercises.
3. Refer to the answer document to check your results and understanding.

## Objective

To explore Ensembl data resources and learn how they can support genetic variant interpretation.
